# Run 01 — Google AI features and your website

**Input:** https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features  
**Why it matters:** The audience needs a reliable baseline for AI Overviews and AI Mode guidance.  
**Audience:** SEO and content leads  
**Estimated elapsed time:** 9 minutes (Gather 3, Synthesize 2, Draft 2, Review 1, Format 1).  
**Review decision:** PASS

## Evidence ledger

- Google describes AI Overviews and AI Mode as experiences that surface relevant links and can use query fan-out across related searches.
- Google says a page must be indexed and eligible to appear in Search with a snippet; it says there are no additional technical requirements for AI features.
- Google recommends the same foundational SEO practices, including crawl access, internal linking, textual content, and accurate structured data.
- Search Console reports AI-feature traffic within the Web search type.

## Output

### AI search visibility still starts with Search fundamentals

Google’s current guidance treats AI Overviews and AI Mode as extensions of Search rather than a separate optimization system. Both experiences can surface supporting links while exploring complex queries, but the underlying eligibility bar remains Search indexing and snippet eligibility.

- AI Overviews help users get the gist of complex questions and explore supporting sites.
- AI Mode can issue related searches across subtopics before presenting a response.
- Google says there are no additional technical requirements beyond being eligible for Search with a snippet.
- Crawl access, internal links, readable text, page experience, and accurate structured data remain useful fundamentals.
- Performance is included in Search Console’s Web search type.

**Implication (inference):** Teams should prioritize content clarity, crawlability, and measurement before creating an AI-specific markup checklist. The practical test is whether important pages are indexable, understandable, and worth citing.

**Source:** [Google Search Central — AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)
